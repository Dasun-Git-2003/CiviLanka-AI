# Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) & RDA Infrastructure Repair Specifications

Standard Operating Procedures, Quality Assurance, and Material Guidelines for Municipal Assets

---

## 1. Pothole & Road Surface Repair Standard Procedure

### Step 1: Defect Marking & Perimeter Cutting

- Mark a rectangular boundary around the pothole extending at least 150mm into sound, unaffected pavement.
- Cut vertical edges to a minimum depth of 50mm using an asphalt saw cutter. Feathered or tapered edges are strictly prohibited as they cause premature edge raveling.

### Step 2: Excavation & Base Preparation

- Remove all loose asphalt, debris, and contaminated sub-base material down to a firm, solid foundation.
- If sub-base is wet or degraded, excavate an additional 150mm and compact dense Graded Aggregate Base Course (ABC) in layers not exceeding 100mm.
- Ensure compaction reaches minimum 98% Maximum Dry Density (MDD) via plate compactor.

### Step 3: Tack Coat Application

- Thoroughly sweep and clean the excavation with a stiff broom or compressed air blast to remove dust and standing water.
- Apply cationic slow-setting bitumen emulsion (CSS-1h) uniformly over the entire base and all vertical cut edges at a rate of 0.35 to 0.50 L/m².
- Allow the emulsion to break (turn from brown to black) before asphalt placement (approx. 15–20 minutes in Sri Lanka tropical climate).

### Step 4: Asphalt Placement & Compaction

- **Cold-mix asphalt**: Permissible for emergency repairs or potholes under 2 m² in area during wet weather. Compact in maximum 40mm loose lifts with hand rammer or vibratory plate.
- **Hot-mix asphalt**: Required for all main arterial roads (Class A & B) and areas exceeding 2 m². Lay at 135°C to 150°C and compact immediately with a tandem steel roller (minimum 5 passes) until no wheel marks remain.
- Finished surface must be flush with surrounding pavement (tolerance: ±3mm).

## 2. Water Distribution Mains Rupture & Leak Repair (NWSDB Protocol)

### Assessment & Isolation

1. **Critical/Emergency Ruptures**: Isolate nearest sectional sluice valves immediately to prevent road foundation undermining.
2. Provide temporary bypass or water bowser supply to affected residents if outage exceeds 6 hours.

### Repair Methodology by Pipe Material

- **uPVC Pipe Circumferential Crack / Small Pinhole (< 50mm)**:
  - Clean pipe exterior down to bare plastic.
  - Install a stainless steel full-circle repair clamp (SS304) with nitrile rubber gasket, torqued to 65 Nm.
- **uPVC Pipe Longitudinal Split / Shatter (> 50mm)**:
  - Saw-cut damaged section squarely.
  - Insert new piece of Class 1000 uPVC pipe connected on both sides with electrofusion couplings or mechanical universal couplings.
- **Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe Joint Failure**:
  - Replace aged EPDM rubber gasket and bolt mechanical collar clamp.
- **Bedding & Backfill Mandatory Requirement**:
  - Backfill with washed river sand minimum 150mm under, around, and over the pipe barrel before placing granular aggregate backfill. Never drop sharp rocks or hard soil clods directly on uPVC pipes.

## 3. Stormwater Drainage & Canal Desilting Guidelines

### Routine Maintenance vs Structural Collapse

- **Silt Accumulation (> 30% reduction in drain depth)**:
  - Classify as Moderate priority. Requires manual or suction excavation of silt and sludge.
  - Discard excavated silt immediately to municipal disposal sites; do not heap along roadside shoulders as tropical rains will wash it back into the drain.
- **Broken / Missing Drain Cover Slabs**:
  - Classify as High priority due to pedestrian hazard and road vehicle wheel drop risk.
  - Install precast reinforced concrete slabs (Grade 30 concrete, minimum 100mm thickness) capable of sustaining 40 kN wheel load.
- **Wall Spalling or Collapsed Rubble Masonry**:
  - Dewater trench section. Rake joints to 25mm depth and repoint with 1:3 cement mortar, or rebuild collapsed masonry section using 1:4 cement mortar with 75mm weep holes spaced at 1.5m intervals.

## 4. Streetlighting & Electrical Circuit Standard

### Safety & Earthing Regulations

- All public street lighting poles must have an individual copper-bonded earth electrode (minimum 16mm dia x 1.5m length) with earth resistance not exceeding 10 Ohms.
- Use only IP66 rated LED luminaires equipped with 10kV surge protection devices (SPDs) to withstand Sri Lanka monsoon lightning strikes.
- Underground cable joints must be enclosed in waterproof resin-filled junction kits.
