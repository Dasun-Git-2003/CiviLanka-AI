# Municipal Infrastructure Asset Degradation & Predictive Risk Standards

> **Reference Standards**: CIDA Asset Management Manual, RDA Bridge & Highway Inspection Guidelines, NWSDB Pipeline Condition Index (PCI).

---

## 1. Structural Condition Assessment Scale

- **Condition Tier 1: Good (Risk Score: 0–25)**
  - Normal operational condition with minimal cosmetic wear.
  - Failure Likelihood: Negligible.
  - Inspection Frequency: Annually or Semi-annually.
  
- **Condition Tier 2: Fair / Moderate (Risk Score: 26–50)**
  - Noticeable surface weathering, minor hair cracking, minor valve leakage.
  - Failure Likelihood: Low to Moderate within 12–24 months.
  - Inspection Frequency: Quarterly.

- **Condition Tier 3: Poor (Risk Score: 51–75)**
  - Significant section loss, structural concrete spalling, heavy rust on ductile iron, repeating joint leaks.
  - Failure Likelihood: High within 3–6 months if left untreated.
  - Inspection Frequency: Monthly.

- **Condition Tier 4: Critical (Risk Score: 76–100)**
  - Severe structural fracture, imminent collapse of retaining wall or culvert, major hydraulic loss.
  - Failure Likelihood: Imminent (within days or weeks).
  - Inspection Frequency: Weekly or Continuous Monitoring.

## 2. Colombo Environmental Degradation Accelerators

1. **Coastal Salinity & Marine Air** (Galle Face, Kollupitiya, Bambalapitiya, Mutwal within 1.5 km of ocean):
   - Accelerates steel rebar corrosion and spalling by **2.5x**.
2. **Monsoon Groundwater Table Saturation** (South-West Monsoon: May–September; North-East Monsoon: November–February):
   - Weakens road subbase bearing capacity; water pressure causes joint displacement in old cast-iron pipes.
3. **Heavy Container Trailer Vibration** (Grandpass, Dematagoda, Orugodawatta freight corridors):
   - Causes micro-fractures in concrete culvert slabs and subterranean water siphon mains.
