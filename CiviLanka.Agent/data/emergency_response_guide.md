# Sri Lanka Municipal Emergency Response, Prioritization & Cost Contingency Framework

---

## 1. Severity Levels, Impact & Target Response Times

### Level 1: Critical (Immediate Danger / Major Disruption)

- **Examples**:
  - Main trunk water pipeline burst (>150mm DI/uPVC) causing street flooding and pressure collapse.
  - Deep road sinkhole or pavement crater (>100mm depth) on main highway/bus route.
  - Collapsed drainage culvert threatening building foundations.
  - Fallen energized streetlight pole or exposed live conductor.
- **Target Response**: Dispatch contractor within 2 hours. Repair completion within 12–24 hours.
- **Cost Multiplier**: Apply +20% emergency surcharge to standard BSR labor and plant rates for night work, temporary dewatering, traffic police coordination, and safety signage.

### Level 2: Poor (High Risk / Escalating Damage)

- **Examples**:
  - Medium water leakage from distribution pipe causing localized road sub-base weakening.
  - Potholes (depth 50mm - 100mm, area > 1 m²) on secondary municipal roads.
  - Broken stormwater drain cover slab on pedestrian walkway.
  - Non-functional streetlight cluster (3 or more consecutive dark poles) in high-crime or pedestrian junction.
- **Target Response**: Dispatch contractor within 24 hours. Repair completion within 48–72 hours (2–3 days).
- **Cost Multiplier**: Standard BSR unit rates with 10% contingency for site preparation.

### Level 3: Moderate (Maintenance Required / Monitoring)

- **Examples**:
  - Minor pipe weeping or damp ground near distribution valve.
  - Surface cracking (alligator cracking), raveling, or minor depressions (< 50mm depth).
  - Heavy silt build-up (> 30% reduction) in stormwater drain causing slow rainwater runoff.
  - Single flickering or dead LED luminaire on suburban street.
- **Target Response**: Inspection within 3 days. Scheduled maintenance repair within 7–14 days.
- **Cost Multiplier**: Standard BSR unit rates without emergency markup.

### Level 4: Good (Fully Functional)

- Regular operational status. Next scheduled preventive inspection in 6 months.

## 2. Standard Cost Estimation Formula for Municipal Repairs

The total estimated repair cost for any municipal infrastructure work order must be calculated as:

$$\text{Total Cost (LKR)} = \text{Material Cost} + \text{Plant/Machinery Cost} + \text{Labor Cost} + \text{Safety \& Traffic Management} + \text{Contingency}$$

Where:

1. **Material Cost**: Sum of each item's quantity multiplied by the CIDA/BSR unit rate in LKR.
2. **Plant/Machinery Cost**: Daily equipment hire multiplied by operational days required.
3. **Labor Cost**: Combined sum of skilled and unskilled labor mandays multiplied by daily wage rates.
4. **Safety & Traffic Management**: LKR 8,000 to LKR 15,000 per day for traffic cones, retro-reflective barrier tape, warning blinkers, and flagmen on public roadways.
5. **Contingency / Preliminaries**: 10% for Moderate/Poor conditions; 20% for Critical emergency repairs.
