# Sri Lanka Municipal Hazard Classification & SLA Triage Matrix (CMC / RDA / NWSDB)

> **Authority**: Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) Works Department, Road Development Authority (RDA), National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB)  
> **Application**: Automated citizen report triage, risk scoring, response scheduling, and emergency dispatch.

---

## 1. Hazard Categories & Priority Classification

### Category A: Pothole & Road Surface Damage (RDA / CMC Highway Maintenance)
- **Defects**: Asphalt depression, crater, alligator cracking, shoulder erosion, gravel subsidence.
- **Urban Corridors**: Galle Road (A2), Baseline Road (A1), High Level Road (A4), Kandy Road (A1).
- **Severity Evaluation**:
  - **CRITICAL**: Diameter > 1.0m or Depth > 100mm on dual carriageway or high-speed arterial road. Imminent risk of vehicular rollover or fatal two-wheeler loss of control.
  - **HIGH**: Diameter 0.5m–1.0m or Depth 50–100mm on bus routes or commercial collector roads.
  - **MEDIUM**: Diameter 0.3m–0.5m or Depth 30–50mm on local municipal streets.
  - **LOW**: Minor surface raveling, shallow depression < 30mm on residential access roads.
- **SLA Windows**:
  - CRITICAL: Emergency cold-patch repair within **2 to 4 hours**.
  - HIGH: Standard hot-mix asphalt patching within **12 to 24 hours**.
  - MEDIUM: Scheduled repair within **48 to 72 hours**.
  - LOW: Routine road maintenance within **7 calendar days**.
- **Crew & Equipment**:
  - Standard Crew: 3 workers (1 Roller Operator, 2 Asphalt Rakers).
  - Machinery: Baby Roller (1–2 ton) or Plate Compactor, Asphalt Cutter, Bitumen Sprayer.

---

### Category B: Potable Water Distribution Leakage (NWSDB)
- **Defects**: Subterranean pipe rupture, distribution joint burst, leaking water service ferrule, arterial transmission collapse.
- **Severity Evaluation**:
  - **CRITICAL**: Main distribution trunk rupture (> 160mm uPVC / Ductile Iron), roadway subbase washout, or continuous potable water flooding threatening structural stability of adjacent buildings.
  - **HIGH**: Rider main leakage (90mm–110mm) causing pressure drop across an entire Grama Niladhari division.
  - **MEDIUM**: Defective consumer service pipe connection (20mm–32mm) on roadside verge.
  - **LOW**: Slow seepage at boundary meter box without soil erosion.
- **SLA Windows**:
  - CRITICAL: Emergency isolation valve closure & repair within **2 to 4 hours**.
  - HIGH: Repair within **12 to 18 hours**.
  - MEDIUM: Repair within **24 to 48 hours**.
  - LOW: Service connection replacement within **5 calendar days**.
- **Crew & Equipment**:
  - Standard Crew: 4 workers (1 Certified Pipe Fitter, 1 De-watering Pump Operator, 2 Trench Excavators).
  - Machinery: Heavy-duty diesel de-watering pump, mechanical trench cutter, hydraulic pipe clamp.

---

### Category C: Stormwater Drainage & Canal Obstruction (CMC / SLLRDC)
- **Defects**: Collapsed culvert slab, missing drain grate, siltation blockage, blackwater overflow during monsoon showers.
- **Severity Evaluation**:
  - **CRITICAL**: Canal or culvert collapse causing localized flash flooding across arterial road or hospital/school entrance during monsoon rainfall.
  - **HIGH**: Missing storm-drain grate on high-traffic pedestrian walkway or main street (imminent pedestrian injury risk).
  - **MEDIUM**: Silted roadside open drain with sluggish water flow.
  - **LOW**: Debris accumulation in secondary residential side drain.
- **SLA Windows**:
  - CRITICAL: Emergency clearing & suction dispatch within **2 to 4 hours**.
  - HIGH: Grate replacement or safety barricade placement within **6 to 12 hours**.
  - MEDIUM: De-silting crew deployment within **48 hours**.
  - LOW: Scheduled municipal canal dredging within **7 calendar days**.
- **Crew & Equipment**:
  - Standard Crew: 4 to 6 workers (Gully bowser crew, manual desilters).
  - Machinery: High-pressure jetting truck, Vacuum Gully Bowser, Mini Excavator.

---

### Category D: Street Lighting & Electrical Hazards (CEB / LECO / CMC Electrical)
- **Defects**: Fallen electric pole, exposed live cable, knocked-over streetlight pole, dangling service wire.
- **Severity Evaluation**:
  - **CRITICAL**: Exposed live conductor touching roadway, water puddle, or accessible to pedestrians. Imminent electrocution danger.
  - **HIGH**: Total blackout of major junction traffic signals or consecutive dark streetlights (> 5 poles) on main highway.
  - **MEDIUM**: Individual burned-out LED luminaire on municipal road.
  - **LOW**: Defective decorative lamp or non-urgent photocell timer replacement.
- **SLA Windows**:
  - CRITICAL: Emergency utility isolation and de-energizing within **1 hour**; site secured within **2 hours**.
  - HIGH: Traffic signal repair within **4 hours**; street line restoration within **12 hours**.
  - MEDIUM: Lamp replacement within **48 hours**.
  - LOW: Non-critical repair within **5 calendar days**.
- **Crew & Equipment**:
  - Standard Crew: 2 to 3 certified electrical linemen.
  - Machinery: Aerial bucket truck (Cherry Picker), insulation tester, safety barrier kit.

## 2. Colombo Urban Risk Multipliers

When evaluating risk and urgency score (1–100), apply the following weighted municipal factors:
1. **School / Hospital Zone Proximity** (within 300m): **+25 points** (Elevate priority by one tier).
2. **High-Speed Arterial Corridor** (Galle Road, Baseline Road, Marine Drive, High Level Road): **+20 points**.
3. **Monsoon / Heavy Precipitation Alert**: **+15 points** for all drainage and road depression incidents.
4. **Pedestrian Density Factor** (Pettah Central, Fort, Town Hall, Bambalapitiya): **+15 points** for open drains/manholes.
