# Sri Lanka Municipal Infrastructure & Construction — Building Schedule of Rates (BSR 2024/2026)

Source: CIDA (Construction Industry Development Authority) & National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) & Road Development Authority (RDA) Standards
All unit rates and costs are expressed in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR).

---

- **100mm Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe (Class K9)**: LKR 14,500 per linear meter.
- **150mm Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe (Class K9)**: LKR 18,800 per linear meter.
- **200mm Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe (Class K9)**: LKR 24,500 per linear meter.
- **250mm Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe (Class K9)**: LKR 31,000 per linear meter.

- **Mechanical Repair Clamp (110mm - 160mm, Stainless Steel SS304 single band)**: LKR 16,500 per unit.
- **Mechanical Repair Clamp (200mm - 250mm, Stainless Steel SS304 double band)**: LKR 28,000 per unit.
- **Electrofusion Coupler / Mechanical Flange Adaptor (110mm)**: LKR 12,500 per unit.
- **Electrofusion Coupler / Mechanical Flange Adaptor (160mm)**: LKR 19,000 per unit.
- **Sluice Gate Valve (100mm dia, PN16 resilient seated)**: LKR 42,000 per unit.

### Earthwork & Trench Excavation for Utilities

- **Manual excavation in ordinary soil up to 1.5m depth**: LKR 2,400 per m³.
- **Mechanical backhoe excavation in roadway trench**: LKR 1,750 per m³.

- **Aggregate Base Course (ABC) backfilling and vibratory plate compaction**: LKR 4,200 per m³.
- **Selected river sand bedding and surround (150mm thickness around pipe)**: LKR 5,800 per m³.
